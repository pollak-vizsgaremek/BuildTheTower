# Képi elemek

- `public/images/construction.png`: a beépített image_gen eszközzel generált, helyben tárolt háttér (nem külső kép-URL).
- `src/components/BuildingMark.jsx`: saját SVG épületjel a mellékelt referenciákhoz igazítva.
- Ikonok: `lucide-react`; a Google beállítása után a tényleges belépési gombot a Google Identity Services rendereli.

A háttér végleges generálási promptja:

> Create a photorealistic construction website background image, portrait aspect ratio 3:4. Close-up bright yellow orange construction hard hat in the LOWER LEFT foreground on a light worktable, with rolled white architectural blueprints extending to the right. Behind, softly defocused concrete building under construction and a tower crane, warm sunrise. UPPER HALF of image is almost empty pale cool lavender-white sky fading softly into the construction scene in the lower half, suitable for navy text overlay. Helmet and papers occupy bottom quarter. Refined realistic commercial photography, pale airy luminous light, orange accents. No text, no logos, no UI, no borders, no watermarks.
